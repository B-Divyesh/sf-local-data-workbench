Privacy,
in plain ink.

Local Data Workbench is designed so your data files do not need our trust. The application processes source files, previews, recipes, and exports on your computer.

What stays on your device

Filenames, file contents, column names, profiles, transformation steps, recipe files, exports, and recent work are not transmitted to Sociobot. We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, crash reporters, or remote logging in the app.

What may leave your device

EventData sentWhy
License verificationThe license token onlyConfirm a one-time purchase, at most once per day.
PurchaseDetails you enter in hosted checkoutSociobot/Dodo acts as merchant of record and issues a license.
Website requestStandard server request dataDeliver this website and installer files. No behavioral analytics are embedded.

Storage and deletion

The license token and its latest verification verdict are stored in local application storage. Recipes and exports are stored only where you choose. Remove the application data or uninstall the app to remove the token; delete recipe and export files using your operating system.

Third parties and rights

Dodo Payments, through the Sociobot billing service, processes purchases under its own privacy terms. GitHub hosts public release downloads. We do not sell personal information. For access, deletion, or privacy questions about purchase records, contact privacy@sociobot.in.

Changes

Material changes will update the effective date and release notes. We will not silently add file-content telemetry.
